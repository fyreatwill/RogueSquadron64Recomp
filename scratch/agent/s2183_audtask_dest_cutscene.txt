s2183 offline image-DMA destination versus cutscene-heap census
Date: 2026-08-23

Method
------
Parsed every [audtask-image-join] and [audtask-dma-range] row in the four
existing logs named by NEXT.md. For each image join, the half-open interval
[dest, dest+len) was intersected with a four-byte interval at each named
physical address:

  heap0       0x25A580
  slot_word0  0x0CA748
  slot_plus50 0x0C7B0C
  vec3_dest   0x165E40
  match_v0    0x0CDB58
  match_a0    0x2231EC

The first two logs therefore record image DMA only at the title and hangar
origins. Their 19 unique `(dest,len)` pairs do not intersect any of the six
cutscene words. The s2181 f2 log later reaches a 640 VI at `0x006A2900`
after hangar, but it has no image-join row for that phase. The `=0` zero
count is the expected stub behavior from the existing PCM/image contract.

Discriminator outcome
---------------------

- `H-AudioDmaHitsCutscene` is refuted for the two completing `=1` logs with
  positive controls.
- `H-AudioDmaElsewhere` is refuted for all recorded image joins in those logs.
- `H-AudioDmaTitleHangarOnly` is supported at the completing-log scope, but
  is not selected for all `=1` runs because the failing log has no same-run
  image-DMA positive control.
- `H-CoverageFailure` is selected for the failing s2179 log only: its 640
  phase is present, but its image-DMA instrument is absent. That zero cannot
  distinguish “no cinematic DMA” from “instrument did not run.”

This is an offline census, not a writer attribution. No guard, skip, or
writeback mitigation follows. The next discriminating run must capture a
failing `ROGUESQ_AUDIO_UCODE=1` cinematic with `[audtask-image-join]` count
greater than zero before classifying its 640-window destinations.
