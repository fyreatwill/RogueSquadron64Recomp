# Owner opt-in settings (one page)

Everything here is OFF by default; unset = retail behaviour. Run from `build/` with the ROM beside it:

```sh
cd build && env ROGUESQ_FPS60=1 ./RogueSquadron64Recomp            # steady 60 fps (s6703)
cd build && env ROGUESQ_FPS60=1 ROGUESQ_DRAW_DISTANCE=2 ./RogueSquadron64Recomp   # more terrain, ~60 fps at 4:3 (s6776/s6810); at 16:9 see the s6902 note below
cd build && env ROGUESQ_FPS60=1 ROGUESQ_DRAW_DISTANCE=3 ./RogueSquadron64Recomp   # far terrain, ~60 fps at 4:3 (s6776/s6810). The s6789-s6790 "0 dropouts" did not match the owner playtest; see the note below
```

Freezes (s6902): the freezes of a second or more that then caught up like buffering should be gone, at every draw distance, with nothing to set. Our own debug bookkeeping grew on every triangle and never shrank, so the game stopped to reorganise it, each pause about twice as long as the last, and memory climbed about 4.7 MB/s (1.7 GB after 5 minutes). Same build, dd4: 4 pauses up to 1.4 s before, none over 46 ms after, and a 9 minute run across 4 mission starts had none. `ROGUESQ_SHADE_ORD_RESET=0` restores the old behaviour. Tell us if you still see a freeze in flight.

Speed at 16:9 (s6902, Liberation of Gerrard V, FPS60, test logging): the "~60 fps" figures above are 4:3. Game frames per second measured: dd1 59, dd2 42-46, dd4 about 35-42. With `ROGUESQ_RDP_ASYNC=1 ROGUESQ_RDP_NODRAIN=1` added, dd4 runs about 53-58. The s6901 far terrain fix costs about 5-7 frames/s at dd4 without those two settings and about 3-5 with them, because it draws more terrain on every heading (`ROGUESQ_DD_FWDWIN=0` trades that terrain back). dd3 at 16:9 was not measured.

Draw distance above 1 (2026-11-05): the game now gets 12 MB of memory instead of 8 MB, because the final mission ran out and crashed at the start. `ROGUESQ_OSMEM_MB=8` restores the old size.

Draw distance above 1 (s6896): large terrain sheets at dd4 are fixed by a change that is on by default above dd1 (`ROGUESQ_G5B_TG_FIX=0` restores the old path). dd3 and dd4 now both show none. Whether this cures the drops you see needs your flight.

Draw distance above 1 (s6901): distant terrain no longer halves depending on which way you face, and no longer cuts out for a moment as you turn through a diagonal. On by default above dd1 (`ROGUESQ_DD_FWDWIN=0` restores the old path). This is the best candidate yet for the terrain drops you see, but only your flight can confirm that. It draws more terrain, so tell me if dd4 feels slower. Measured on Liberation of Gerrard V only.

Draw distance above 1 (s6904): extended terrain now fades into the haze instead of drawing clear and then turning solid fog colour at a line. On by default above dd1, off at dd1. `ROGUESQ_DD_FOG=0` restores the old look. `ROGUESQ_DD_FOG=<start>,<end>` sets how far the fade starts and ends, as multiples of the level's own fog distances (defaults at dd4: 1.5,3.2). No frame rate cost measured at level 5 dd2 over 300 s.

Draw distance above 1 (s6899): the water and ground that vanished in the Liberation of Gerrard V cutscene at dd4 are back, by a change that is on by default (`ROGUESQ_DD_EYEWIN=0` restores the old path). It did not measurably change flight, so it is not yet known to help the in-flight terrain drops. Measured at 16:9 only.

Fixed (s6898): the black block on the right in a Liberation of Gerrard V cutscene (X-wing, then TIEs over a tower) at 16:9 is gone at draw distances 1 and 4. `ROGUESQ_G1D_LETTERBOX=0` brings it back. Known issue: at draw distance 4 that cutscene still loses the dark water and most of the ground (docs/owner_sheet_s6898_dd1_dd4_scene.png).

Knife lines (s6906): long thin lines shooting in from the screen edge are removed by a change now on by default at every draw distance, including 1 (`ROGUESQ_W_CLAMP=0` restores them).

Draw distance above 1 (s6895): single flat-sky frames at dd2 and dd4 are fixed by a change that is on by default there (`ROGUESQ_W_CLAMP=0` restores the old path). dd3 and dd4 still have a separate fault: large wrong terrain sheets in some places, which show as black stepped gaps in the scene after a failed mission. dd2 had none in any test.

Wide + far terrain (s6882): `ROGUESQ_ASPECT=21:9 ROGUESQ_DRAW_DISTANCE=4` ran at 6-9 game fps before this fix and now runs 20-31 (lvl1, 135 s of flight, test logging on, no FPS60). FPS60 with wide + dd4 has not been measured since the fix.

Wide + far terrain (s6883): a further fix on by default takes `ROGUESQ_ASPECT=21:9 ROGUESQ_DRAW_DISTANCE=4` to 30 game fps with test logging, and with `ROGUESQ_FPS60=1 ROGUESQ_RDP_ASYNC=1 ROGUESQ_RDP_NODRAIN=1` and no logging to a median of 60 (dips to the 40s; one lvl1 run). `ROGUESQ_G1D_SPANCELL=0` restores the old behaviour. Please judge the look: docs/owner_sheet_s6883_spancell.png.

Ultrawide speed (s6884): at 21:9 far terrain, `ROGUESQ_FPS60=1` needs `ROGUESQ_RDP_ASYNC=1 ROGUESQ_RDP_NODRAIN=1`; without them the game stays at 30 frames/s (44 with them but debug logging variables set, 60 in normal play). Adding `ROGUESQ_RDP_SCREEN_JOB=1` (test, off by default) removed the game's dips to the 40s on lvl1 (two pairs of runs, same picture). The game runs at a steady 60, but what reaches the screen has not been shown to be any smoother yet, so please say if it feels different. Other levels and 4:3 are not tested yet.

Mission crash fix (s6893): far terrain could overrun a small game buffer and corrupt whatever sat next to it, the likely cause of the Gerrard V crash. Fixed by default, `ROGUESQ_TILEPOOL=0` restores the old behaviour. Please re-fly Gerrard V past half way to confirm.

Combos: FPS60 + DRAW_DISTANCE=2 held 300 s clean on levels 1 and 5 (s6262, alive_at_end=1, 0 stalls, 0 crash lines). FPS60 + DRAW_DISTANCE=4 at 4:3 flew 300 s clean on levels 1, 5 and 10 (s6865-s6867). Times in this paragraph that say "held" count from a harness gate; real flight seconds are in [G1-TRUE-FLIGHT.md](G1-TRUE-FLIGHT.md). 21:9 + FPS60 + DRAW_DISTANCE=4 (the ultrawide bundle) ran clean on levels 9 (261 s of flight) and 17 (275 s over 5 mission restarts) with the radar disc in its ring and nothing displaced (s6857, repeated on the fixed memory layout in s6859, docs/owner_sheet_s6859_region_21x9_fps60_dd4_spillhi.png; levels 0-8, 10-16 and 18 then loaded and flew 52-129 s clean on that layout in s6860, level 5 for 148 s; level 10 HUD edge anchoring fixed in s6860, docs/owner_sheet_s6860_lvl10_hud_ab.png). 16:9 + FPS60 + DRAW_DISTANCE=4 (the TV bundle) ran clean on levels 1 (253 s of flight) and 9 (s6858, docs/owner_sheet_s6858_region_16x9_fps60_dd4.png); builds before s6858 crashed at mission load if ROGUESQ_OSMEM_MB=12 was added at 16:9 with a draw distance above 1 (fixed: the draw-distance spill memory now sits above the enlarged heap). Widescreen + FPS60 (16:9 and 21:9, lvl1, s6264; needs TITLE_ORIGIN='0x[0-9A-F]*' MISSION_RE='[0-9A-F]+'): flight reached at width=796/1024, ~205 s hold (not the full 300 s), alive_at_end=1, 0 VI stalls, 0 crash lines.

TV (16:9): `cd build && env ROGUESQ_ASPECT=16:9 ROGUESQ_FULLSCREEN=1 ./RogueSquadron64Recomp`. Ultrawide: same with `ROGUESQ_ASPECT=21:9`. Any screen: `ROGUESQ_ASPECT=auto ROGUESQ_FULLSCREEN=1`.

Rules of thumb: combine settings freely only after checking the table. Kill the game with SIGKILL.

Ultrawide frame order (s6885): at 21:9 the game was showing frames slightly out of order and skipping some, which can look like judder. Fixed, on by default; nothing to set. `ROGUESQ_WIDE_EARLY=0` restores the old behaviour so you can compare the feel.

Lower display lag (s6886, try it and judge the feel): add `ROGUESQ_PRESENT_INFLIGHT=2`. At 4:3 it takes about 25 ms off the time a finished frame waits to reach the screen (72 -> 48 ms) and still shows 60/s. At 21:9 far terrain it removes the worst waits rather than the typical one. Do not use 1: it halves the picture to 30/s. Hiding, minimizing, resizing and the fullscreen toggle are all checked and keep working (s6887). Moving the window to another display is not checked yet.
